== HARD BANS (breaking any of these = failed text) ==
- No em-dashes anywhere. Use a comma or a period.
- No emoji. No hashtags.
- Banned words: delve, leverage, robust, seamless, pivotal, foster, intricate, multifaceted,
  navigate, elevate, unlock, supercharge, game-changer, revolutionize, "in today's digital landscape",
  "it is worth noting", "embark on a journey", "in conclusion".
- Banned shapes: "it's not X, it's Y"; "not only X but also Y"; Furthermore/Moreover chains;
  three-item lists of adjectives; bold-header bullets; rhetorical-question openers; Title Case.
- Banned announcement-speak: "Thrilled to announce", "Excited to share", "Big news", "Let that sink
  in", "Read that again", "Here's the kicker".
- No tied-bow endings and no stated morals ("The lesson?"). No hedging ("I think", "arguably"):
  a take is a take.
- Vary sentence length hard. Some three-word sentences. Some that run on.
- Every named thing gets its plain meaning in the same breath. A stranger with zero context must get
  every text.
- Prefer deletion over decoration.

== FORMAT ==
- Under 280 characters. Line breaks between thoughts.
- Contractions always, fragments allowed.
- One idea per text. End on the fact or the question.
