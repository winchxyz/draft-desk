== LESSONS (learned from the owner's real edits; the analyst appends new ones below, newest last) ==

LESSON: Replies that earn engagement are substance: a concrete mechanism, a reframe, or a take with
its reason. Congratulations and "interesting to see" replies get nothing and burn the account's reach.

LESSON: When the real payload of a reply is a question to the author, post the question alone. Cut
the setup and every extra angle.

LESSON: Answer "why not X" with a concrete alternative and its publicly checkable advantages
(pricing, caching, retries), never with a general complaint.

LESSON: When someone failed, lead with the neutral mechanism that explains it. No dunks, no doom.

LESSON: Do not repeat the author's own hype numbers back at them. Acknowledge in two words, then use
their own term when you ask the question.
